# APCO building and site: what the street view photos show

Photos in `source/streetview/` (Google Street View, 2025-2026).

## Building (for the 3D model)
- Two-story office building, likely a former bank (drive-thru canopy at the NE).
- Ground floor: red-brown brick. Second floor: continuous bronze/amber reflective
  glass curtain wall in long horizontal bands with dark bronze mullions and a dark fascia.
- Brick stair/elevator tower centred on the west (front) face, taller than the roof line,
  with a white tenant sign; main entrance at its base (photos 04, 05).
- South/east end: second floor clad in dark brown vertical metal panels with narrow
  vertical windows; small dark awning over a side door (photos 07, 08).
- NE: lower brick and glass annex connecting to the drive-thru canopy (white fascia,
  yellow trim), with a tall pylon sign on two posts next to it (photos 01, 08).
- Height: about 28-32 ft to the top of the curtain wall; tower a few feet higher (estimate).

## Site details
- West front: two ADA spaces with blue symbols and a hatched access aisle between them,
  next to the entrance tower; concrete wheel stops along the building row; heavy
  alligator cracking in front (photo 05). This is the "Restripe handicap" item.
- Islands: curbed mulch islands with clipped shrubs at row ends; the west island is grass
  with trees in mulch rings (photo 03); Route 130 strip is grass with mature trees (01, 02).
- Light poles: square "shoebox" fixtures on tall poles, in islands and along rows.
- Municipal Dr strip: grass with mature trees, no curb cut in front of the building;
  the only Municipal Dr entrance is at the SE corner, with a planted island at its west side (09).
- Brick dumpster enclosure with green dumpsters on the east side (08).
- Striping: white single-line stalls, faded; heavy cracking in the south and west aisles (05, 06).
